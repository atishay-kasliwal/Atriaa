Atria · Raleigh region

Every waiting room, watched after triage.

Triage captures how a patient looks at the door. Atria keeps watching while they wait: it reads each new vital and observation, flags sustained change, and tells the charge nurse who may need a second look, with the evidence. These are the emergency departments across the Triangle that Atria is built to run in.

02Separates signal from noiseSustained change across signals rises in the queue. One-off spikes are marked transient and settle back.
03Explains every rankingAsk Atria answers in plain language and cites the actual values, times and queue version.
