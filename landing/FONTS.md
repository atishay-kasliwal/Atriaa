# Switzer

Switzer is by Indian Type Foundry, distributed through
[Fontshare](https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/switzer) under the
[ITF Free Font License](https://www.fontshare.com/licenses/itf-ffl), version 2.0.

The website uses unmodified original WOFF2 files at weights 400, 500, 600, and 700.
The build fetches them directly from Fontshare when they are not already cached,
then self-hosts them for Atria's own web use. Font binaries are excluded from Git;
other users should obtain their own copy directly from Fontshare under its terms.
