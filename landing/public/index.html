A CONTINUOUS PICTURE

One check-in is a moment.
Care needs the whole story.

Atria connects what a patient reports with what the care team observes. Every new reading adds context to the last.

01

Hear the patient.

Check in, share symptoms, and send an update when something feels different. Give patients a way to be heard while they wait.

Patient check-in & updates
02

Notice what changes.

Bring repeat readings into a timeline. See the direction of change, the underlying values, and why a signal appeared.

Trends with traceable evidence
03

Keep people in control.

Nurses review the evidence, request a re-check, and decide on priority. Atria supports that decision and records the action.

Human review at every step

FROM A WEEKEND BUILD TO WHAT’S NEXT

What happens
after triage?

The waiting room keeps changing, even when the queue looks the same.

Built at MLH’s Hacktoberfest Hacks @ All Things Open, Atria connects patient check-in, repeat observations, a nurse’s waiting-room view, and explanations grounded in actual readings.
