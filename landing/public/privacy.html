Your early-access signup.

This notice covers the Atria landing page and its early-access waitlist, maintained by Atishay Kasliwal and the Atria project team.

What you share

The form collects your email address, an optional name, the role you choose, and your agreement to receive early-access updates. We also record when you signed up and the version of that agreement. Please don’t include patient records, medical details, or other sensitive information.

What we use it for

We use these details to manage interest in Atria and contact you about product progress, early access, and opportunities to give feedback. Joining this list does not create a product account or grant access to a clinical service. We do not sell the signup list.

Where the details are stored

The landing page runs on Cloudflare Workers. Signup details are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database accessible to authorized project maintainers. Cloudflare processes network information to serve and protect the website. The signup handler uses your IP address for temporary rate limiting; it does not store it in the waitlist table.

Your choice

You can ask us to correct or remove your signup, or stop sending you updates, by contacting Atishay through the contact links on his GitHub profile. Don’t post your email address in a public issue. We keep signup information while running the early-access program, unless you ask for removal sooner.

The prototype is separate

Links to the Atria demo and GitHub open separate services. This notice describes the landing-page waitlist only. The current Atria demo uses synthetic patient information and is not validated for clinical use.
